All orders are packaged in plain, discreet outer boxes with no product identification visible on the outside. Products are packed in temperature-controlled, leak-proof, tamper-evident inner packaging to preserve product integrity during transit.

Not happy with your order? Contact us within 30 days of delivery and we'll make it right — no questions asked.

Return Eligibility

Unopened products: Eligible for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. Products must be in original, sealed packaging. Opened products: Eligible for a replacement or store credit within 30 days if the product is defective, damaged, or significantly different from the description.

How to Initiate a Return

1. Email us at support@topeptides.com with your order number and reason for return.
2. We'll respond within 1 business day with a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and instructions.
3. Ship the item back using a trackable method. Keep your tracking number.
4. Once we receive and inspect the return, we'll process your refund within 3–5 business days.

Refund Processing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Please allow 3–5 business days for processing after we receive your return. Credit card refunds may take an additional 5–10 business days to appear on your statement depending on your bank.

Non-Returnable Items

Items that have been opened and used (except where defective), items returned without an RMA number, and items not in original packaging are not eligible for return.
