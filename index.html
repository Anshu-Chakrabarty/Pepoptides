Custom Peptide Synthesis

Synthesise custom peptides to exact specifications using Fmoc solid-phase synthesis with rigorous QC at every stage.

  • Sequences from 2 to 100+ amino acids
  • Standard & non-natural amino acids
  • N/C-terminal modifications
  • Isotope labelling (¹³C, ¹⁵N, ²H)
  • Fluorescent & biotin conjugates
  • Purity ≥95%, 98%, or 99% options
02

Recombinant Protein Production

High-quality recombinant protein expression and purification for your unique research targets.

  • E. coli, yeast & mammalian systems
  • His-tag, GST, MBP fusions
  • Affinity & size exclusion chromatography
  • Endotoxin removal available
  • SDS-PAGE & Western blot QC
  • Bulk scale-up capability
03

Aggregation & Fibril Services

Expert preparation of preformed fibrils and amyloid aggregates for Parkinson's and Alzheimer's research.

  • Alpha-synuclein PFFs (50–80 nm)
  • Tau PFFs (2N4R, 0N3R, etc.)
  • Beta-amyloid fibrils (1-40, 1-42)
  • ThT fluorescence validation
  • TEM characterisation available
  • Seeding-competent validated batches
